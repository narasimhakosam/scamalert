Top 5 Scams Targeting College Students

Scammers prey on student anxieties around internships, tuition fees, and part-time income. Learn their common tricks:

1. The "Internship Registration Fee" Scam

The Trick: You receive an SMS/WhatsApp claiming you have been shortlisted for an internship at a top MNC, but must pay a "security deposit" or "training fee" (₹499 – ₹2,500) within 15 minutes.
Reality: Real companies pay you for internships. They never charge application or registration fees.

2. The "Telegram Daily Task / Like Videos" Scam

The Trick: "Earn ₹2,000–₹5,000/day by liking YouTube videos or writing Google reviews. Join Telegram @daily_task." They pay ₹150 for initial tasks, then ask you to "invest" in prepaid tasks to unlock higher payouts.
Reality: Once you deposit larger sums, the Telegram channel blocks you.

3. The "Fake Scholarship Disbursement" SMS

The Trick: "Your government merit scholarship of ₹25,000 is approved. Click bit.ly/scholarship-claim to link Aadhaar/UPI."
Reality: The phishing link mimics a banking portal to capture your UPI PIN and credentials.

4. Delivery Package / Electricity Disconnection Panic

The Trick: "Your India Post parcel is held due to incomplete address. Update at parcel-tracking.xyz within 2 hours or return to sender."
Reality: Creates artificial panic to rush you into submitting credit card or personal details.

AI Model & Detection Architecture

Student ScamGuard AI uses a hybrid detection pipeline combining ML classification with deterministic rule engines for maximum explainability:

1. Trained NLP Classifier (Scikit-Learn + TF-IDF)

Trained on the UCI SMS Spam Collection dataset and curated student scam corpora using Logistic Regression with character/word n-grams. Outputs calibrated ham vs spam probabilities.

2. Deterministic Scam Indicator Engine

Scans for student-specific threat markers: Upfront payment requests, OTP/credential harvesting, artificial countdown timers, guaranteed selection promises, and brand impersonation. Produces exact character offsets for evidence highlighting.

3. URL Intelligence & Link Heuristics

Extracts embedded URLs, evaluates URL shorteners (bit.ly, tinyurl), raw IP addresses, suspicious TLDs (.xyz, .top, .buzz), and look-alike brand domains.

4. Explainability & Action Mapping

The system never gives an unexplainable "black-box" decision. Every point in the 0–100 risk score traces directly back to a detected signal with matched verbatim text quotes and tailored defensive guidance.
